What this means for the decisionno cost figures — none exist in the data

A call is worth making when its cost is below the customer's churn risk, measured as a share of what a save is worth.
Calling everyone pays whenever a call costs less than 30.5% of a save.

Calling only the flagged pays whenever a call costs less than 36.8% of a save.
The model moves that bar by 6.3%, and gives up 15 of 54 leavers to do it. Unless a call costs between 31% and 37% of a save, the list does not change the decision.
